## Key finding: the load effect (evidence)
Controlled comparison between free writing and sentence-building with ideas supplied (Satzbau units 1 and 2):
- **With ideas supplied (no idea work):** participial attributes (2 rounds), Konjunktiv II forms (hätte/wäre + Partizip II), indirect speech forms (gehe, sei, es gebe), genitive after wegen/trotz, infinitive group as subject, "Ich wäre eher überzeugt, wenn … würde" all mostly correct.
- **In free writing:** the same structures often break (Konjunktiv II in wenn-clauses three times, verb position in dass- and relative clauses, relative pronoun with preposition).
- **Conclusion:** knowledge is largely there. The problem is attention and automation under load. Strategies in GER_CONTEXT.md ("Writing strategy") target this.
- **Chunks transfer:** once given as a fixed chunk ("Ich wäre eher überzeugt, wenn …"), it was used correctly in the next exercise.
- **Still wrong even without idea load:** relative pronoun with preposition (bei deren / auf den / von die), plural agreement ("Preisen", "Beamten … arbeite"), logic of irreal sentences (restating the fact instead of the opposite), als/als ob + Konjunktiv II (once "als versteht er alles").

**Observations on how the learner learns**
- Applies most feedback on rewrite, but points explained once often don't stick yet (als vs. wenn, Perfekt with sein).
- Verb position slips mainly when paraphrasing or fronting phrases. Free writing is better.
- Picks strategically useful C1 phrases and idioms; sentences about own life stick better than generic ones.
- Mistakes cluster on neuter -ment nouns (das Argument) and loanwords (der Job, die Tools, die Tests).
- In free first drafts, new vocabulary from feedback sometimes replaces correct earlier wording (Kinderspiel → "Spaßveranstaltung"). Don't swap what already works.

**Handy rules (given once, not drilled):**
- Nouns on -ment are mostly neuter (das Argument; exception: der Moment). Loanword plurals take -s (Tools, Tests, Jobs).
- Relativpronomen = Artikel, except Dativ Plural "denen" and Genitiv "dessen/deren". Three steps: Präposition → Kasus, Bezugswort → Genus/Zahl, Pronomen wählen.
- Irreal = Gegenteil der Wirklichkeit. Positives wird negativ, Negatives wird positiv.
- Konjunktiv I: Stamm + -e (er gebe), sein: er sei. If identical with Indikativ (sie arbeiten), use Konjunktiv II (sie arbeiteten).
