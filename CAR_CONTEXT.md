# CAR: Context (stable background)

> Public, sanitized version. No names, employer details, finances or personal circumstances.
> Scope: job-search strategy only. Visa/relocation admin and language learning live in separate chats.

## Role of the assistant
Career handler: optimize the search, challenge weak assumptions, draft outreach and CV content, run interview prep, keep the plan tied to visa timeline and runway. Style per CAR_STYLE_GUIDE.md.

## Profile
- Software engineer, ~3 years, large cloud company. Backend (Java, C++, Python), cloud, APIs, AI integration; intermediate JS/TS.
- Degree recognized in Germany (anabin H+).
- Target: software / full-stack engineer, mid-to-senior, hybrid or remote.
- German: roughly B2.

## Situation
- Relocating to Germany on the Chancenkarte (12-month job-seeker visa).
- Bottleneck: recruiters hesitate on candidates not yet in Germany; interviews stall on location.
- Runway covers the full visa period without income.

## Rules to VERIFY (public info, may change)
- Chancenkarte: up to 20 h/week of work and trial employment of up to 2 weeks; self-employment not allowed.
- Blue Card 2026 thresholds: EUR 50,700 standard / EUR 45,934.20 reduced; contract of at least 6 months.
- Chancenkarte is not tied to a city.

## Strategy: tracks
- A: Employer-internal route via contacts. Passive unless concrete requisitions appear.
- B: Open market, referral-first, job-first workflow (find the req, then pick 2-3 contacts).
- C: Remote roles at German employers (German entity needed for sponsorship).
- D: Bridge employer (consultancy or mid-size firm) to convert to a Blue Card quickly.
- E: Two-week trial work once in Germany, used when a process stalls on location.
- Effort split: B 50%, C 20%, D 20%, A 10%.
- Calendar: December is slow, January-February stronger. Bridge-job gate roughly month 6 of the visa.

## Principles
- Outreach: one specific ask per contact; personalize the first line; no mass messages.
- Referrers do not need to live in the target city.
- Never share confidential figures or interview questions in public materials.
- Part-time work only as a lever when savings buffer drops, preferring a Minijob (~10 h/week) over a 20 h job.

## Tools
- LinkedIn archive CSV tiered locally by company and role (script keeps data on the user's machine).
- Outreach templates: close contact, weak tie, recruiter, re-engage a stalled process, informational chat.
